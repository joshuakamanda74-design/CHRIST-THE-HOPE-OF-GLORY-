About Our Church

Christ the Hope of Glory is a Christian church in Matinka, Makeni, Sierra Leone, led by Pastor Edward Kargbo.

Our desire is to preach the Gospel of Jesus Christ, lead people toward salvation, strengthen believers in faith, and show God's love to our community.

Our Mission

To preach the Gospel and encourage people to follow Jesus Christ.

Our Vision

Lives transformed through God's Word, faith, prayer, and love.
